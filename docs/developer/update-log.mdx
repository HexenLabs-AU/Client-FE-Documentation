Follow the development of Fleet Empire, from new vehicles and destinations to company management improvements and bug fixes.

<Note>Fleet Empire is in alpha. These entries record changes at the time of release; later updates may replace earlier values or rules.</Note>

<Update label="October 11, 2026">

### Changes

- Reworked vehicle purchase system.
- Added Mexico, unlocked at level 15.
- Added an inventory system for cosmetics and crates. Crates were announced as coming soon.
- Added a new truck brand across all vehicle types.
- Added vehicle liveries.
- Added a corporation quick chat board with options limited by Roblox's Terms of Service.
- Doubled maximum corporation contract sizes.
- Fleet Managers can now rebuild engines.
- Added a Financial Management tab to the Bank, allowing you to choose whether bills and purchases use cash or bank funds.
- Added a 2× EXP boost to the shop.
- Started the Halloween event, ending November 2, 2026.
- Added a limited-edition Halloween driver with an increased chance of a Mythic specialty.
- Made quality of life improvements.
- Optimized server performance.
- Reworked AUS/UK driver rerolling.
- Increased Mexico payouts by 20%.

### Fixes

- Fixed minor bugs.

</Update>

<Update label="October 4, 2026">

### Changes

- The Lowboy now supports autopilot.
- Capped driver meeting prices at $200,000.
- Capped driver specialty reroll prices at $2,000,000.
- Added Mythic driver specialties.
- Driver safety meetings now increase overall driver safety by 25 points per meeting.

<Update label="October 3, 2026">

### Changes

- Announced batches of 50 businesses listed in the marketplace every five hours, with 450 businesses available through this release. This brings the planned total to 600; subsequent purchases must be made from other corporations.
- Updated mileage-based breakdown thresholds: 0% below 100,000 miles; 15% from 100,000 to below 400,000 miles; 30% from 400,000 to below 800,000 miles; and 45% at 800,000 miles or above.
- Optimized servers to reduce lag.
- Added a filter for corporation contracts.
- Unlocked the Lowboy truck. Color options were announced as coming soon.
- Added the **Heavy Haul License** to Upgrades, available at level 20 and above.
- Added a Pilot Truck for escorting oversized loads.
- Added escort crew staff, available to hire at level 20.
- Tanker load payouts increased for state to state deliveries.
- Changed Heavy Tanker and Tanker Tractor prices.
- Rental listings now warn you when you are about to rent a vehicle with high mileage.
- You can now return your rental vehicle early for a 2% fee.
- When looking at corporation contract requests, you can now view the corporation before accepting/denying them.
- Corporation reputation is no longer locked at 0, and now has the ability to go negative.
- Corporation invites now expire after 48 hours if not accepted.
- Advanced Dispatcher game pass owners can now prioritize specific contracts through Dispatch Rules.
- Drag the map by holding the left mouse button, and zoom with the mouse wheel.

### Fixes

- Fixed minor bugs.
- Fixed corporation contracts disappearing during page refreshes and increased the refresh rate.

</Update>

<Update label="October 1–2, 2026">

### Changes

- Added 100 corporation businesses, bringing the total to 150. Further additions were initially paused for two weeks.

- Decreased corporation tanker delivery payouts.
- You can now manually dispatch corporation contracts.
- All corporation contracts now have a fixed six-hour deadline.
- EO and Executive roles must now apply for a contract on the board and wait for the other corporation to accept it.
- Doubled maximum corporation contract sizes.
- Limited contracts sent to the same corporation to two every 24 hours.

### Fixes

- Fixed corporation contracts not completing when overdue.
- Fixed minor bugs.

</Update>
