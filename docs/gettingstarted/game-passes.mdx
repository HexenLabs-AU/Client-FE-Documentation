Game passes add optional features. Open **Shop → Passes** to compare them, and check the Roblox confirmation prompt for the current price in **Robux**.

## Compare features

| Pass or shop item | Benefit shown in game |
| --- | --- |
| VIP | 20% more delivery cash and EXP, plus a VIP badge on your company card. |
| Extra Drivers | Three additional driver slots, on top of company roster upgrades. |
| Autopilot | Adds a map switch so your personal truck picks up and delivers loads automatically, with no hired-driver wage taken. |
| Advanced Dispatcher | Adds more dispatch rules, including weather, freight waits, rates, vehicle size, and fuel or repair holds. Open the controls from Staff. |
| Double Luck | Rolls Federal Contracts and staff candidates twice and keeps the better result. Odds panels show the adjusted chances. |
| Liveries | Two Tone, Stripes, and Flames patterns in your chosen colors for your trucks. |

## Where to use the features

- **Autopilot:** the map switch for your personal truck.
- **Advanced Dispatcher:** **Staff → Dispatcher → Advanced Controls → Open Rules**.
- **Liveries:** preview during dealership configuration or use the eligible truck's livery control in Fleet.
- **Extra Drivers:** check the increased capacity in your staff roster.
- **Double Luck:** check the odds panel when reviewing supported rolls.

Cash packs and temporary boosts are covered in [Shop and boosts](/docs/gameplay/shop-and-boosts).
