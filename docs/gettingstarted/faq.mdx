## Getting started

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where should I start?">
    Open **Fleet → Dealership → Small Vehicles → Cargo Van → Purchase**. Open **Headquarters** and assign the van to **yourself**. Select **Your Truck** in **Dispatch → Manual Dispatch**. Review a load, select **Dispatch Load**, and wait for delivery. Rewards are credited when the load is delivered. You can hire staff later as you expand.

    Follow [Start your company](/docs/gettingstarted/how-to-play) for preparation and [Dispatch](/docs/gameplay/dispatch) to learn how to compare and review freight.

    <Frame caption="Select Your Truck for your first delivery. This example shows an established company’s hired driver.">
      <img
        src="/images/guides/dispatch-load.png"
        alt="Manual Dispatch review with the Dispatch Load button"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why can't I buy a vehicle or use financing yet?">
    Availability depends on the requirements shown in your game. Some equipment needs a license. Truck financing requires a **credit score of at least 500**, and its availability also varies with company level and upgrades.

    Check [Company upgrades and licenses](/docs/gameplay/upgrades-and-licenses) and the current dealership or Bank offer rather than assuming one level or price applies to every company.

    <Frame caption="Check your branch’s license requirements in Upgrades → Licenses.">
      <img
        src="/images/guides/licenses.webp"
        alt="Equipment license cards with company-level requirements"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Where do I redeem codes?">
    Open **Settings**, find **Redeem Code**, enter the code, and select **Redeem**. Check [Codes](/docs/gettingstarted/codes) for announced rewards and driver-slot requirements.

    <Frame caption="Find Redeem Code in Settings.">
      <img
        src="/images/guides/settings.webp"
        alt="Settings page with code redemption controls"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why are drivers so expensive?">
    Building a large fleet is meant to be a long-term progression goal. Driver prices increase with each driver you hire, so expanding your company takes planning.

    Fleet Empire was designed as a semi-idle management game. Building a large fleet is intended to take time, rather than happen in the first month of the game's release.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Money and offline play

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why were my trucks repossessed?">
    **Missing two consecutive loan or financing payments causes trucks to be taken to cover the loan. There is no set order for which trucks are taken.**

    Loan and financing payments follow your settings in **Bank → Financial Management**, including the selected source and automatic fallback. Keep enough funds available through those settings to cover payments while you're offline.

    Delivery earnings from offline work go into **escrow**. You claim those earnings when you come back online, so they aren't available to cover payments while you're away.

    **Offline loan and finance payments are not capped.** Payments continue for as long as you're offline.

    Before logging out, review the **Loan & Financing Payments** source in Financial Management and keep enough accessible funds to cover the time you expect to be away.

    For corporation member loans, **two failed payments trigger repossession**. See [Corporation loans](/docs/gameplay/corporation-management#review-member-loans) for requesting terms and approval.

    <Frame caption="Check the Loan & Financing Payments source before going offline.">
      <img
        src="/images/guides/payment-sources.webp"
        alt="Financial Management payment-source controls"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I repay a loan or truck financing early?">
    Yes. You can repay loans and truck financing early, but **the full precalculated interest is still payable**. Early repayment does not reduce that interest.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does bank savings interest continue offline?">
    Yes. Bank savings earn **1% every three real hours** on the **first \$250,000,000** of your bank balance, including while you are offline.

    <Frame caption="The Bank panel shows the next interest payment.">
      <img
        src="/images/guides/bank-interest.png"
        alt="Bank savings interest rate and interval"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do contracts work offline?">
    **No contracts progress offline.** Business deals, Federal Contracts, and corporation contracts all require online progress.

    Ordinary automatic freight dispatch can still work offline. See [Contracts](/docs/gameplay/contracts#offline-contract-progress).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## About the game

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What inspired you to make this game?">
    I'm a truck driver and a part-time software engineer. While playing Roblox, I noticed a gap: trucking games focused on driving, with little attention to the business and management side.

    Fleet Empire combines those two passions into a trucking management game.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Was Fleet Empire made with AI?">
    AI has been part of the development process. Around 40% of the scripts were developed with AI assistance.

    As a solo developer, I also use AI to help test for bugs and simulate more than 300 scenarios within minutes. It's one of the tools I use to develop and improve the game.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why isn't Fleet Empire completely realistic?">
    Although I have real-world trucking experience, matching every part of the game to real life would leave most players bankrupt or close to it.

    I've adjusted the gameplay to balance challenge and enjoyment, with players aged nine and above in mind.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why is development slow?">
    Fleet Empire is primarily developed by me, alongside a full-time job outside of development.

    I work on the game whenever I can, normally spending more than 40 hours a week on it. I'd rather release a well-tested update than rush something out.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
