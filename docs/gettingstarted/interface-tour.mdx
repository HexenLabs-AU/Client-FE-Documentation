The sidebar connects every part of your company. Use this page when you know what you want to do but are not sure where to find it.

## Choose a menu

| Menu | What you use it for |
| --- | --- |
| Map | See freight locations, trucks, hubs, weather, and active deliveries. |
| Headquarters | Check company progress, shared fuel, your personal truck, branches, and storage. |
| Corporation | Work with other companies, businesses, contracts, and a shared vault. |
| Fleet | View vehicles, browse the dealership, and upgrade vehicle parts. |
| Dispatch | Review loads or manage automatic dispatch priorities. |
| Marketplace | Browse player rentals and auctions. |
| Staff | Hire drivers, dispatchers, fleet managers, and escort crew. |
| Contracts | Review business offers, Federal Contracts, and relationships. |
| Bank | Move funds, review financing, track performance, and choose payment sources. |
| Insurance | Compare coverage and review claims or risk. |
| Upgrades | Improve your company and view licenses. |
| Shop | Browse optional passes, cash purchases, and boosts. |
| Leaderboard | Compare companies across several rankings. |
| Logs | Check recent company activity and transactions. |
| Settings | Adjust audio, motion, notifications, codes, and company details. |

## Read the top bar

The top bar shows **Cash**, **Bank**, **Fuel**, **Reputation**, and **Fleet**. Cash and bank funds are separate balances. The **Claim Rewards** control is also available here.

A delivery status bar can show your personal truck's destination, cargo, payout, and remaining time. The game month and any current conditions appear nearby.

## Explore the map

Hold the left mouse button to drag the map and use the mouse wheel to zoom. Select a state to see freight waiting there. The **State Names** control changes whether names appear on the map.

Use the legend to distinguish hubs, contracts, headquarters, your truck, company drivers, and toll booths. Weather markers identify areas affected by conditions such as rain or storms.

The **Active Deliveries** cards show routes, progress, remaining time, and delivery status. Check these when you want to see what your drivers are doing.
