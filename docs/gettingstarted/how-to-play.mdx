title: "Start your company"
description: "Buy your first cargo van, hire a driver, and prepare to find freight."
---

Your first goal is to get a vehicle and a driver ready for work. Start with a cargo van, then learn where to check fuel, find freight, and review a job.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Buy a cargo van">
    Open **Fleet → Dealership → Small Vehicles → Cargo Van → Purchase**.

    Review the purchase price before buying. The dealership also offers customization and financing; you can explore those when you are ready to expand.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Hire a driver">
    Open **Staff** and roll a **Day Labor** driver. Use **Assign vehicle** underneath to assign your van.

    When reviewing a candidate, check their **Driver Cut**, speed, safety, and fuel bonuses. The driver cut is the share of delivery earnings the driver receives.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your company">
    Open **Headquarters → Overview** to check the shared fuel reserve. Your fleet draws from this reserve.

    Use **Staff → Drivers** to check your driver and assigned vehicle. A busy or broken truck cannot take a new manual load in the recorded interface.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find freight">
    Open **Dispatch → Manual Dispatch**. Select an available driver, then review the **Load Board**.

    Compare payout, distance, freight type, and toll costs. Selecting a load opens **Review**, where you can check the assigned truck, estimated time, and the amount you keep after the driver's cut.
  </Step>
</Steps>
