Start by buying a cargo van and assigning it to yourself. Your first deliveries help you learn the load board before you hire drivers and expand your fleet.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Buy a cargo van">
    Open **Fleet → Dealership → Small Vehicles → Cargo Van → Purchase**. Review the purchase price before buying.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign the van to yourself">
    Open **Headquarters** and assign your first vehicle to **yourself**. It becomes **Your Truck**, which represents your personal vehicle in Dispatch.

    Check **Headquarters → Overview → Your Truck** to confirm your assigned vehicle and review its condition. Check the shared fuel reserve before taking work.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your personal truck">
    Open **Dispatch → Manual Dispatch**. In the **Driver** stage, choose **Your Truck** when it is available. A busy or broken truck cannot take a new manual load.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose and review freight">
    In **Load Board**, compare freight type, route, distance, payout, and toll costs. Select a suitable load to open **Review**.

    Check the assigned truck, pickup location, estimated time, payout, and any tolls. Available loads vary; the example below does not guarantee a particular route or reward for a new company.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Dispatch the load">
    Select **Dispatch Load** at the bottom of the review screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for delivery and payment">
    Wait for the vehicle to deliver the load. The delivery timer and map show its progress. **Delivery rewards are credited when the load is delivered.**

    Once the truck is available again, you can review another load.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Expand when you are ready

After learning the delivery flow, you can hire drivers to operate additional vehicles. Open **Staff**, roll a **Day Labor** driver, then use **Assign vehicle** underneath. See [Hire and manage drivers](/docs/gameplay/drivers) for regional hiring differences.
