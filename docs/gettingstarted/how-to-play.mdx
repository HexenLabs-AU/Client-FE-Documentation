## 3. Build your fleet and team

You can buy trucks, rent vehicles, and hire drivers as your company grows. Fleet Empire is a semi-idle management game, and building a large fleet is a long-term progression goal.

A useful approach is to learn the delivery process before expanding. Decide whether your next purchase should be a vehicle or another driver based on what you need for your company.

For announced reward codes, see [Codes](/docs/gettingstarted/codes).

<Warning>
Keep an open driver slot before claiming the `500K` code, which awards a Legendary driver.
</Warning>

## 4. Check your finances before logging out

If you have loans or financed vehicles, make sure your **Bank** has enough money to cover payments while you're away.

| Money or payment | What happens while you're offline |
| --- | --- |
| Offline earnings | Go into escrow and are claimed when you return. |
| Bank balance | Must be sufficient to cover your offline loan and finance payments. |
| Loan and finance payments | Continue while you're offline, without a time cap. |

<Warning>
Offline earnings do not go straight into your Bank. They cannot cover payments while you're away.
</Warning>

The **Financial Management** tab in the Bank lets you choose whether bills and purchases use cash or bank funds. Read [Why were my trucks repossessed?](/docs/gettingstarted/faq) for the offline payment explanation.

## 5. Understand toll costs

Some states charge tolls per axle. Count the axles on both your truck and trailer, rather than individual tires.

For example, a truck with three axles and a trailer with two axles has five axles total.

On the Pennsylvania Turnpike, the rate is **\$60.80 per axle**:

**\$60.80 × 5 = \$304.00**

You can avoid tolls, but your trip will take longer. Check [Tolls](/docs/gameplay/tolls) for the full rate table.

## 6. Explore corporations

Corporations let companies work together, manage businesses, and complete contracts.

To create your own corporation, you need **company level 10 or above** and **\$6,000,000**. A corporation must reach **level 3** before it can buy a business.

Read [Corporations](/docs/gameplay/corporations) for taxes, fuel coverage, business production, contracts, and member loans.
