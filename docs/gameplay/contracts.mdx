title: "Business and Federal Contracts"
description: "Understand offers, accepted deals, relationships, and Federal Contract listings."
---

Open **Contracts** to switch between **Offers**, **Federal**, and **Relationships**. These are separate from contracts posted through your corporation.

## Review business offers

The **Offers** tab contains work offered by businesses. Each offer shows the commodity, quantity, pickup and destination, distance, estimated loads, delivery rates, completion bonus, and expiry.

Compare the full workload with your fleet before using **Accept**. The interface also provides **Deny** and counteroffer controls. Denying an offer removes it from the offer list.

**Agreed Deals** displays work you have already accepted. Check these commitments before taking on more freight.

## Track business relationships

The **Relationships** tab shows business names, relationship scores, and trust labels such as **Preferred Carrier**. This is where you can review businesses your company works with.

Check the current offers rather than planning around a guaranteed arrival time.

## Offline contract progress

<Warning>
  **No contracts progress offline.** This includes business deals, Federal Contracts, and corporation contracts. Stay online to progress contract deliveries.
</Warning>

## Explore Federal Contracts

The **Federal** tab provides **Roll Contracts**, **Odds**, **How It Works**, and views for **My Contract**, **Board**, and **History**.

Board listings show the host company, route, required freight and equipment, deposit, level requirement, participant space, and fleet-capacity requirement. A recruiting listing offers **Join**; full listings are identified in the board.

Read **How It Works** and the listing requirements before joining or rolling a contract. 

### When you get paid

<Note>
  **Federal Contracts pay only when the contract ends. You do not receive a payment after each load.**
</Note>

The loads your company completes during the contract count toward your contribution. At the end of the contract, your payout is based on how many loads your company completed.

Keep enough money available for running costs while the contract is active, since the contract payout arrives at the end.
