Open **Settings** for audio, display behavior, codes, tutorial lessons, and company details.

## Adjust your experience

| Control | What you can change |
| --- | --- |
| Music Volume | Music loudness. |
| Sounds Volume | Sound-effect loudness. |
| Reduce Motion | Motion effects. |
| Do Not Disturb | Hide incoming call popups. |
| Tutorial Lessons → Open Lessons | Revisit in-game lessons. |

If you hide call popups, remember to check your fleet and logs for breakdowns.

## Redeem a code

Find **Redeem Code** in Settings, enter a code in **Enter Code**, then use **Redeem**. The [Codes](/docs/gettingstarted/codes) page lists announced rewards and any preparation needed.

## Change your company profile

**Company Logo** offers logo choices used on your company card and leaderboard.

**Company Name** has a **Rename** control. The recorded rules require **3–24 characters** and allow **one free rename every 30 days**. Check the current availability shown before renaming.

## Understand the danger zone

<Warning>
  **Delete Company** permanently resets company progress, trucks, staff, contracts, cash, bank funds, and loans. This is a reset action. Read the confirmation carefully before using it.
</Warning>
