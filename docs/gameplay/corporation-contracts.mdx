Corporation contracts turn stored commodities into delivery work. Open **Corporation → Contracts** or use **Create Freight Contract** from the warehouse.

## Choose who can haul the freight

An in-house contract is for your corporation's members. An open contract allows other corporations to apply.

When outsourcing, review how much the haulers earn and how much your vault keeps. Listings show the payout per unit, commodity, route, equipment type, and quantity.

## Posting limits, fees, and deadlines

| Rule | Current limit |
| --- | --- |
| In-house contracts per day | Unlimited. |
| Active contracts posted by your corporation | Three total, across in-house and open contracts. |
| Contracts sent to the same other corporation | Two per 24 hours. |
| Posting fee | 1% of the contract value. |
| Contract deadline | Six hours. |

Track your active contracts before posting another.

## Find work for your corporation

1. Open **Corporation → Contracts → Open Board**.
2. Filter by **All**, **Box/Van**, **Flatbed**, or **Tanker**.
3. Compare the route, remaining freight, equipment, payout share, and time left.
4. Use **Apply** for a suitable contract.

The posting corporation reviews applications. Its CEO or another role with the relevant permission approves hauling access.

## Track active contracts

**Posted by You** shows work your corporation has posted. **Hauling for Others** shows work accepted from other corporations.

Contract cards show progress, remaining time, hauler information, and the split between **Haulers Earn** and **Vault Keeps**. Check progress against the deadline rather than relying only on the size of the payout.
