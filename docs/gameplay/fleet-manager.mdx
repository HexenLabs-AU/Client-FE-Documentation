A fleet manager helps maintain your vehicles. Open **Staff → Fleet Manager** to review candidates and automation controls. You can hire one fleet manager per company.

## Compare candidates

Candidates appear in **Temp Agency**, **Fleet Recruiter**, and **Executive Search** recruiting lanes. Hiring is available immediately. Check the hire cost, monthly salary, and roadside arrival-time benefit. **Fleet-manager salaries are paid every game month, or once per real hour.**

## Set maintenance automation

| Control | What the interface offers |
| --- | --- |
| Auto Refuel | Refill the shared fuel reserve when it reaches your chosen threshold. |
| Auto Repair | Repair worn parts on idle trucks at your chosen condition threshold. |
| Auto Rebuild | Rebuild engines to help maintain trucks with substantial mileage. |

Review each control's explanation before enabling it. Automation can create expenses, so keep money available through the relevant [payment sources](/docs/gameplay/bank#choose-payment-sources).

## Offline and personal-vehicle maintenance

**Automated maintenance works while you are offline and covers your personal truck.** Keep funds available for the enabled automation.

Rental fuel, maintenance, and repairs are the **renter's responsibility**. See [Rental responsibilities](/docs/gameplay/marketplace#understand-rental-terms-and-responsibilities).

## Respond to breakdown calls

A breakdown call identifies the affected truck and provides **Open Fleet**. Use that link to inspect the vehicle. **Got It** dismisses the notification.

**Settings → Do Not Disturb** hides incoming call popups. If you enable it, use Fleet, Staff, and Logs to check for problems yourself.
