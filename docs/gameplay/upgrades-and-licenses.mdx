Open **Upgrades** for company-wide improvements and equipment licenses. These are separate from [individual vehicle upgrades](/docs/gameplay/vehicle-upgrades).

## Compare company upgrades

| Upgrade | Purpose shown in the interface |
| --- | --- |
| Expanded Credit | Increase truck-financing slots. |
| Fleet Discretion | Reduce police and accident exposure. |
| Reinforced Fuel Tanks | Expand the shared fuel reserve. |
| Maintenance Bay | Improve breakdown and repair costs. |
| Roster Expansion | Add driver seats. |
| Route Optimization | Improve trip times. |

Check the current tier, unlock requirement, price, and benefit before purchasing. New companies start with **three financing slots**, with a maximum of **eight**. **Both company levels and Expanded Credit upgrades add slots.** Financing requires a **credit score of at least 500**.

## Check equipment licenses

**USA and Mexico have separate company upgrades and licenses.** Check the branch you are managing before planning purchases.

Open **Upgrades → Licenses**. The available licenses are:

| License | Minimum company level | Equipment category |
| --- | --: | --- |
| Flatbed | 3 | Open deck / flatbed equipment. |
| Tanker | 5 | Liquid / tanker equipment. |
| Heavy Haul | 20 | Lowboy / oversize / heavy-haul equipment. |

**Licenses are permanent within the branch where you purchase them.**

License cards show the company-level requirement and whether your company is licensed. Licenses authorize the matching equipment; check the dealership or rental listing for its additional requirements.

Check the current license card for its purchase price before planning a purchase.
