Open **Fleet** to view your vehicles or browse the dealership. Begin with a cargo van; expand when your company can support the purchase and running costs.

## View your vehicles

**My Vehicles** shows vehicle names, plates, equipment types, star ratings, condition, location, mileage, and capacity. **View Details** opens an individual vehicle's information.

## Browse the dealership

1. Open **Fleet → Dealership**.
2. Choose **New Trucks** or **Used Trucks**.
3. Browse **Small Vehicles**, **Mid Size Vehicles**, or **Large Vehicles**.
4. Select a vehicle and review its configuration and price.

To buy your starter van, follow **Small Vehicles → Cargo Van → Purchase**.

Some equipment needs a license or company progression before it becomes available. Check the in-game requirement rather than assuming every vehicle is available immediately.

## Configure a vehicle

The purchase interface includes brand selection and separate **Engine**, **Transmission**, **Brakes**, and **Tires** choices. The plus and minus controls change part selections, and the interface shows overall stars and vehicle statistics.

You can also choose **Cab Color** and **Wheel Color**. Available livery previews include **None**, **Two Tone**, **Stripes**, and **Flames**. The interface allows you to preview liveries and indicates when the Liveries pass is needed to keep one on a truck.

Review the final configuration before using **Purchase**. **Finance** is available only for eligible **new dealership vehicles**.

## Understand financing availability

**Only new vehicles purchased from the dealership can be financed. Used trucks and auction purchases are not eligible.** Financing requires a **credit score of at least 500**.

New companies start with **three financing slots**, with a maximum of **eight**. **Both company levels and Expanded Credit upgrades add slots.** Check **Bank → Truck Financing** and the vehicle's current finance offer before committing.

Financing creates ongoing payments. Compare the upfront amount, installment, rate, term, and total repayment displayed in the offer; the docs do not treat one recorded company's terms as a universal rate.

For an alternative to buying, see [rentals and auctions](/docs/gameplay/marketplace).
