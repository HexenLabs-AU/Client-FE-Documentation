Businesses give a corporation commodities to transport. Open **Corporation → Businesses** to view production, storage, and available purchases.

## How a business works

1. Buy a business when your corporation is eligible and has a free slot.
2. Production adds commodities to the warehouse continuously.
3. Create freight contracts to move stored commodities.
4. Watch upkeep and inactivity timers to keep the business operating.

## Unlock and expand business ownership

Your **corporation must reach level 3** before it can purchase a business. This is the corporation's level, separate from your personal company's level.

| Capacity | Business slots |
| --- | --: |
| Starting capacity | 1 |
| Maximum after upgrades | 3 |

Use **Corporation → Upgrades → Business License** to expand business capacity. Check the next upgrade's requirements and cost in game.

## Read the business dashboard

The dashboard shows total business value, total production, business count, and warehouse usage. Commodity rows show production and stored amounts against capacity.

Business cards show location, cargo category, efficiency, production, value, upkeep, and activity-related timers. Watch these timers when deciding which freight to move next.

## Upkeep and inactivity

| Rule | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| Business upkeep | 2% of the business value every 24 hours. |
| Inactivity | Automatically sold after 48 hours with no outbound freight. |

<Warning>
  **A business is automatically sold after 48 hours with no outbound freight.** Keep freight moving out of the business and watch its inactivity timer.
</Warning>

Plan for the recurring upkeep cost when deciding how many businesses your corporation can support.

## Turn stored commodities into work

**Production enters the warehouse continuously**, rather than arriving in one hourly batch. **Create Freight Contract** starts the contract-posting flow for that stored freight. See [Corporation contracts](/docs/gameplay/corporation-contracts) for audience and payout choices.

**One game month equals one real hour.** A production rate labeled per month is therefore a rate per real hour. Check the production timer and warehouse capacity when planning freight.

## Browse or sell businesses

The business marketplace filters by **All**, **Box/Van**, **Flatbed**, or **Tanker**. Listings identify the business, location, production, value, and owning corporation. Use **View** to inspect a listing; **Buy** begins its purchase flow.

### Find available businesses

The marketplace restocks **every eight hours**, with **roughly ten businesses** added at a time. Other corporations can also list their own businesses for sale, so player listings provide another source of available businesses.

Watch the restock countdown and check the current listings rather than expecting exactly ten businesses to remain available.

### Business restock prices

| Business category | Restock price range |
| --- | --: |
| Box/Van | \$50,000,000–\$80,000,000 |
| Flatbed | \$60,000,000–\$100,000,000 |
| Tanker | \$100,000,000–\$250,000,000 |

These ranges apply to restocked businesses. Review the individual listing's price before buying; other corporations set their own resale listing prices.

Owned businesses have listing controls including **List Price** and **List**, and may provide **Manage**. Buying, managing, or listing a business requires the relevant corporate permission.

## Improve the corporation

**Corporation → Upgrades** uses corporate vault funds. The interface says upgrade tiers are capped by corporation level.

| Upgrade | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Business License | Business slots. |
| Corporate Charter | Member capacity. |
| Warehouse Expansion | Storage capacity. |
| Freight Contracts | Delivery income benefits. |
| Logistics Network | Delivery speed benefits. |
| Maintenance Alliance | Repair savings. |
| Recruiting Office | Hiring savings. |

Review costs and the next-tier benefit in game. Check the displayed limits, upkeep, inactivity timers, and restock countdown when planning your businesses.
