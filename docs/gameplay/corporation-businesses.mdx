title: "Corporation businesses"
description: "Understand production, warehouse storage, listings, and corporate upgrades."
---

Businesses give a corporation commodities to transport. Open **Corporation → Businesses** to view production, storage, and available purchases.

## Read the business dashboard

The dashboard shows total business value, total production, business count, and warehouse usage. Commodity rows show production and stored amounts against capacity.

Business cards show location, cargo category, efficiency, production, value, upkeep, and activity-related timers. Watch these timers when deciding which freight to move next.

## Turn stored commodities into work

Produced commodities enter the warehouse. **Create Freight Contract** starts the contract-posting flow for that stored freight. See [Corporation contracts](/docs/gameplay/corporation-contracts) for audience and payout choices.

Production is labeled per game month in the interface. Read the production timer rather than treating it as a calendar month.

## Browse or sell businesses

The business marketplace filters by **All**, **Box/Van**, **Flatbed**, or **Tanker**. Listings identify the business, location, production, value, and owning corporation. Use **View** to inspect a listing; **Buy** begins its purchase flow.

Owned businesses have listing controls including **List Price** and **List**, and may provide **Manage**. Buying, managing, or listing a business requires the relevant corporate permission.

## Improve the corporation

**Corporation → Upgrades** uses corporate vault funds. The interface says upgrade tiers are capped by corporation level.

| Upgrade | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Business License | Business slots. |
| Corporate Charter | Member capacity. |
| Warehouse Expansion | Storage capacity. |
| Freight Contracts | Delivery income benefits. |
| Logistics Network | Delivery speed benefits. |
| Maintenance Alliance | Repair savings. |
| Recruiting Office | Hiring savings. |

Review costs and the next-tier benefit in game. Check the displayed limits, upkeep, inactivity timers, and restock countdown when planning your businesses.
