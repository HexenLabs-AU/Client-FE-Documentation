Insurance helps manage certain fleet costs. Open **Insurance** to review your active plan, compare coverage, and see claims.

## Read your active plan

The active-plan panel shows the premium, covered repairs and cargo, collision limit, deductible, and next premium due. A deductible is the portion of an eligible claim that you pay yourself.

Coverage and price depend on the plan and your company's displayed risk. Read the values in your game before changing coverage.

## Compare plans

The recorded choices are **Basic Liability**, **Standard Commercial**, and **Premium Full Coverage**. Plan cards compare premiums, expected coverage, and estimated net cost using the company's current figures.

Use **Select Plan**, **Change Plan**, or **Cancel Coverage** to reach the relevant action. Review any coverage waiting period and confirmation before proceeding.

## Understand risk

The **Risk Profile** includes overall fleet risk, driver safety, accident history, and traffic record. **Why Is My Rate This High?** explains the factors affecting the current quote, including fleet size or value, incidents, driver safety, and traffic history.

The recording also shows a cautious-driving discount. Check the actual quote rather than assuming a fixed discount or premium.

## Review savings and claims

**Insurance Savings** compares total repair costs, the insurer's contribution, and your contribution. **Claims & Liability** lists incident amounts, insurer payments, your payments, and claim status.

Check the current plan for coverage percentages, caps, waiting periods, and eligibility.
