Dispatch connects your drivers to freight. Open **Dispatch** and choose **Manual Dispatch** or **Auto Dispatch**.

| Your goal | Dispatch mode |
| --- | --- |
| Choose a driver and load yourself | [Manual Dispatch](#review-a-manual-load) |
| Let a dispatcher find ordinary freight | [Auto Dispatch](#set-automatic-priorities) |
| Complete contract commitments automatically | Auto Dispatch with **Contracts** priority; stay online. |

## Review a manual load

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a driver">
    In **Manual Dispatch**, the first stage is **Driver**. Use size and cargo filters, then select an available driver. **Your Truck** represents work you drive yourself. Busy vehicles are marked in the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Compare freight">
    The **Load Board** lets you search by cargo or destination. Switch between **Near Driver** and **All Loads**, and sort by **$/mile**, **Payout**, or **Shortest**.

    Read the route, distance, weight, payout, earnings per mile, and any toll charge. Freight also has an equipment type, so the truck must suit the load.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the review">
    Select a load to open **Review**. Check the driver, assigned truck, freight, distance, payout, estimated time, tolls, and pickup location. **You Keep** shows the amount after the driver's cut; fuel and other running costs still matter when planning your company budget.

    Resolve any availability or broken-truck message before proceeding. Then select **Dispatch Load** at the bottom of the review screen. Wait for the vehicle to deliver the load; delivery rewards are credited when it arrives.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Empty trips to pickup

Trucks drive **35% faster while travelling empty to a pickup location**. This speed increase applies to the empty pickup leg, rather than the loaded delivery.

## Set automatic priorities

**Auto Dispatch** allows a dispatcher to find loads for idle drivers. Use the on/off control to enable or stop it. **Manage Dispatcher & Rules (Staff)** opens the staff controls.

Choose a priority:

| Priority | What it asks the dispatcher to favor |
| --- | --- |
| Highest Pay | Higher-paying loads. |
| Best \$/Mile | Earnings relative to travel distance. |
| Short Runs | Shorter trips. |
| Trusted Businesses | Work from businesses your company has relationships with. |
| Contracts | Required priority for automatic contract work. |
| Manual Dispatch | Work you choose manually. |

You can set a company default and use individual driver priorities. **Follow Company** uses the company setting; an individual choice overrides that default.

The optional **Advanced Dispatcher** pass exposes more dispatch rules through the staff menu.

## Check toll preferences

**Auto Dispatch → Toll Booths** shows whether you are paying or avoiding tolls. **Avoid Toll Booths** trades toll charges for longer routes that may use more fuel. See [Tolls](/docs/gameplay/tolls).

## Understand offline work

Automatic dispatch supports ordinary freight while you are offline. **No contract work progresses offline**, including business deals, Federal Contracts, and corporation contracts. See [Contracts](/docs/gameplay/contracts#offline-contract-progress).

## If dispatch cannot start

- **Busy truck:** wait for its current delivery to finish.
- **Broken truck:** inspect the assigned vehicle and its condition.
- **Unsuitable equipment:** choose freight that matches the truck.
- **Contracts not progressing offline:** return online; ordinary freight and contract work follow different offline rules.
