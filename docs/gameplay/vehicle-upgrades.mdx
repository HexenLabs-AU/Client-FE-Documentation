Vehicle upgrades affect individual trucks. Company upgrades live in a separate **Upgrades** menu.

## Check a vehicle's condition

Vehicle cards show overall condition and mileage. The personal truck panel in **Headquarters** also breaks condition down into engine, tires, brakes, and transmission.

When dispatch reports that a truck is broken, follow the interface's **Manage Driver/Truck (Staff)** link to investigate the assigned vehicle. The recorded review screen requires the truck to be idle before taking a load.

## Find the truck you want to improve

1. Open **Fleet → Upgrades**.
2. Search by vehicle name or plate.
3. Use the size and tier filters to narrow the list.
4. Review the truck's individual part controls and current overall stars.

Each truck has separate engine, transmission, brake, and tire selections. The **?** controls provide additional information. Review the available change and its cost before purchasing an upgrade.

The interface also shows **Maxed** vehicles and provides **Add a Livery** for eligible trucks.

## Plan for maintenance

A higher star rating and part condition are different measures: upgrades describe the truck's configuration, while condition shows wear. Check both when comparing trucks.

A [fleet manager](/docs/gameplay/fleet-manager) can help automate refueling, repairs, and engine rebuilds through its controls.

## Find engine rebuilding

Open **Fleet → My Vehicles** and select the truck to find its rebuild option when rebuilding is needed. Review the displayed cost before proceeding.
