Escort crew support oversized and heavy-haul work. Open **Staff → Escort Crew** to see the staff and vehicle assignments used for this work.

## Prepare for heavy hauling

The **Heavy Haul license for Lowboy equipment requires company level 20**. Check its current price under **Upgrades → Licenses**, and review the vehicle and job requirements before expanding into heavy hauling.

**Each Lowboy requires one escort.**

## Manage escorts

The screen shows escort-driver cards with a **Pilot Car** assignment control. It also provides **Hire Escort Drivers**, candidate information, hiring cost, and monthly salary.

Hiring is available immediately. **Escort salaries are paid every game month, or once per real hour.** The Heavy Haul license still requires company level 20.

Use **Let Go** only after reviewing the dismissal confirmation.

## Review the fallback option

**Use escort service when my crew is busy** is an available toggle. Review the job's escort and permit charges when comparing heavy-haul work; these costs affect the budget for the trip.

**Without an available escort, your Lowboy cannot take the larger loads, which reduces earning opportunities.** Use the current load and staff screens to check requirements and charges.
