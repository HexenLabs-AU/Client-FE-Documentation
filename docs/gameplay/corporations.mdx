A corporation brings companies together to manage businesses, share benefits, and complete contracts.

## Start a corporation

| Requirement | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Creation cost | $6,000,000 |
| Minimum company level | 10 |
| Corporation level needed to buy a business | 3 |

After creating your corporation, you can set entry requirements, corporate laws, and permissions for each role.

## Set rules and permissions

The CEO decides which roles can manage the corporation, including who can purchase and upgrade businesses or approve member loans.

### Company tax

Company tax takes your chosen percentage from each member's daily earnings.

**Federal Contract earnings are excluded from company tax.**

### Fuel coverage

When fuel coverage is enabled, members are reimbursed whenever they purchase fuel. Reimbursements come from the corporation's bank account.

Corporations can also pay for tolls. See [Tolls](/docs/gameplay/tolls) for rates and axle calculations.

## Reputation and loyalty

| Measure | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| Reputation | Increases when a contract is completed successfully and decreases when a contract misses its deadline. |
| Loyalty | Members earn 1 loyalty point for every hour they have been in the corporation. Loyalty appears in the member list. |

## Businesses and production

Your corporation must reach **level 3** before purchasing a business.

Businesses can be purchased by the CEO or by someone the CEO has given permission to buy them. You can also purchase businesses from other corporations when they choose to sell.

Businesses produce commodities on a recurring production cycle. Produced commodities go into your warehouse, where you can create contracts to move them.

## Create and accept contracts

From the warehouse, you can make a contract available to your own members or offer it to other corporations.

### In-house contracts

Corporations can post **two in-house contracts per day**. Other contracts must be outsourced.

### Outsourced contracts

When outsourcing a contract, you choose the percentage your corporation keeps for each pallet or gallon delivered. That share goes directly into the corporation's bank account.

If your corporation does not own a business yet, you can still look for work on the corporation contract board and arrange a contract with another corporation.

## Corporation upgrades

Use money earned by your corporation to purchase upgrades that benefit all members.

## Member loans

Corporations can lend members up to the amount they have contributed through **company tax and donations**.

Loans must be approved by the CEO or someone with permission to approve them through management.

<Warning>
A member cannot leave the corporation while they have an active corporation loan. The CEO cannot disband the corporation while a member loan remains outstanding.
</Warning>
