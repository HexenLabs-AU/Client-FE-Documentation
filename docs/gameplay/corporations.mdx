A corporation brings player companies together. Members can move shared freight, contribute to a corporate vault, and benefit from corporation upgrades while retaining their own companies.

<Note>
  Corporations are currently available in USA. Mexico does not have corporations yet.
</Note>

## Start with the main page

Open **Corporation** to find the corporation interface. The **Main Page** shows corporation level, EXP, reputation, specialty, vault funds, and the member list.

| Your goal | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| Create a corporation | Company level 10 or higher and \$6,000,000 to create it. |
| Join a corporation | Its joining requirements, tax, fuel and toll coverage, and your role. |

Review the corporation's rules before contributing money or accepting work.

## Understand what is shared

| System | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| Corporate vault | Holds donations, tax, and the corporation's share of contract income. |
| Businesses | Produce commodities stored in the warehouse. |
| Contracts | Move warehouse freight with your own members or other corporations. |
| Upgrades | Use corporate funds for shared capacity and member benefits. |
| Roles | Control who can manage money, members, businesses, and work. |

Your personal company cash and trucks remain your own.

## Business ownership at a glance

Your **corporation must be level 3** to buy a business. Corporations start with **one business slot** and can increase capacity to **three through upgrades**. See [Corporation businesses](/docs/gameplay/corporation-businesses) for production, upkeep, and marketplace availability.

## Contract posting at a glance

In-house contracts have **no daily posting limit**. A corporation can have **three active posted contracts in total**, across in-house and open work. Posting costs **1% of the contract value**, and each contract has a **six-hour deadline**.

See [Corporation contracts](/docs/gameplay/corporation-contracts) for the workflow and progress controls.

## Reputation and loyalty

| Contract work | Reputation earned |
| --- | --- |
| Pallets delivered for another corporation | +1 per 250 pallets, capped at 30 per contract. |
| In-house contracts and your own business deliveries | 0. |

Previously completed contracts reflect the reworked reputation rules. Missing contract deadlines reduces reputation.

Loyalty appears in the member list. Members gain one loyalty point for each hour spent in the corporation.

Members need **at least 120 loyalty** to receive an offered CEO cash bonus. Bonuses are capped at **\$5,000,000 per member every 12 hours**. See [Member cash bonuses](/docs/gameplay/corporation-management#pay-member-cash-bonuses).

## Borrow carefully

The corporation's Loans screen shows your eligible vault share and repayment terms. Tax paid into the corporation and donations determine the borrowing limit, capped by its current bank balance. Approval requires the CEO or another permitted role.

<Warning>
A member cannot leave while they have an active corporation loan. The CEO cannot disband while a member loan remains outstanding.
</Warning>
