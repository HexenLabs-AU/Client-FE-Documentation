title: "Leaderboards and logs"
description: "Compare company rankings and trace recent activity."
---

Use **Leaderboard** to compare companies and **Logs** to understand activity in your own company.

## Explore the leaderboard

Search for a company and switch between **Overall** and **Weekly** rankings. The interface provides **Deliveries**, **Revenue**, **Miles**, **Reputation**, and **Value** categories.

Choose a category that matches what you want to compare. Revenue and company value describe different measures, so a company's place can change between categories.

Check the current leaderboard for reward details and reset information.

## Check company logs

Open **Logs** and filter by **All**, **Deliveries**, **Money**, **Fleet**, or **Staff**. Entries show dates, activity descriptions, and positive or negative amounts where applicable.

Use these entries alongside **Bank → Recent Transactions** and **Financial Overview** when a balance changes or you need to identify a repair, payroll charge, or delivery payout.

Corporations have their own [activity logs](/docs/gameplay/corporation-management#review-corporation-activity).
