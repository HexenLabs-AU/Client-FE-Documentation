A dispatcher helps organize work for your drivers. Open **Staff → Dispatcher** to review your current dispatcher or available candidates.

## Compare a candidate

The recruiting lanes are **Temp Agency**, **Logistics Recruiter**, and **Executive Search**. Hiring is available immediately. **Dispatcher salaries are paid every game month, or once per real hour.**

Read the candidate's hiring cost, monthly salary, dispatch delay, and listed bonuses before hiring. The current dispatcher panel shows the staff member managing your office.

## Configure Advanced Dispatcher

Open **Staff → Dispatcher → Advanced Controls → Open Rules**. These controls require the **Advanced Dispatcher** pass.

### Choose priorities and contract order

**Dispatch Priority** chooses what an idle driver looks for next. The screen offers Highest Pay, Best \$/Mile, Short Runs, Trusted Businesses, Contracts, and Manual Dispatch. Company and individual priorities are explained in [Auto Dispatch](/docs/gameplay/dispatch#set-automatic-priorities).

**Contract Order** ranks **Corporation Contracts**, **Business Deals**, and **Federal Contracts**. Use the up and down arrows to change the order. Drivers using the **Contracts** focus work through this list from the top.

**Deal Order** sets which agreed business deal they work on first. The list is empty when you have no agreed deals.

### Filter routes and loads

<Accordion title="All route and load filters">
  | Control | What it does |
  | --- | --- |
  | Avoid Storms | Skip loads that start or end in a state currently affected by a storm. |
  | Wait for Return Freight | Wait for a load in the delivery state before leaving empty. |
  | Skip Loads That Lose Money | Require pay after the driver's cut, fuel, and wear to be above zero. |
  | Minimum Lane Rate | Choose Any, Average+, or Top Lanes to filter for better rates. |
  | Minimum Load | Choose Any, Half Full, or Full to control how much of the truck is used. |
  | Maximum Trip Distance | Choose Any, 600 mi, 1,000 mi, or 1,500 mi to limit trip length. |
  | Empty Run to a Pickup | Choose None, 150 mi, 350 mi, or 600 mi. None keeps the driver in their current state. |
  | Driving Style | Choose Cautious, Normal, or Aggressive. Cautious is slower and uses less fuel and wear; Aggressive is faster with more of each. |
  | Keep a Fuel Reserve | Choose Off, 25%, or 50%. A trip must leave the selected share of the shared fuel reserve. |
  | Hold Worn Trucks | Choose Off, 60%, or 75%. Park trucks whose worst part is below the chosen condition until repaired. |
  | Stay in Home Region | Take loads ending in your headquarters' region. |

### Understand rule exceptions

Rules stack: an ordinary load must pass every enabled rule. **Contract work, Federal Contracts, and agreed business deals are commitments and ignore these filters.**

If nothing matches for **60 minutes**, the dispatcher takes the best available load anyway, then applies the holds again. Keep this fallback in mind when using strict filters.

Use **Done** to finish configuring the panel. **Reset to Defaults** restores the default settings.

## Arrange driver meetings

Open **Staff → Dispatcher → Driver Meetings → Schedule Meeting**, then choose the meeting that suits your fleet. Meetings take effect after current deliveries finish.

| Meeting | Benefit shown | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Safety Meeting | Safety Performance +25%; fewer accidents and traffic stops. | 2 hours. |
| Maintenance Meeting | Maintenance Performance +25%; fewer breakdowns and less wear. | 2 hours. |
| Incentive Meeting | Revenue Performance +5%; more money per run. | 2 hours. |

**Meeting effects do not stack.** Review the current cost before scheduling; the screenshot's \$200,000 is an example company price.
