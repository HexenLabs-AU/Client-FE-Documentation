title: "Dispatchers and meetings"
description: "Review dispatcher candidates, salary, rules, and driver meeting controls."
---

A dispatcher helps organize work for your drivers. Open **Staff → Dispatcher** to review your current dispatcher or available candidates.

## Compare a candidate

The recruiting lanes are **Temp Agency**, **Logistics Recruiter**, and **Executive Search**. Their availability depends on the requirement shown in the game.

Read the candidate's hiring cost, monthly salary, dispatch delay, and listed bonuses before hiring. The current dispatcher panel shows the staff member managing your office.

## Open dispatch rules

Use **Advanced Controls → Open Rules** to reach the additional dispatch controls. The interface identifies the **Advanced Dispatcher** pass where it is required.

For company defaults and individual driver priorities, use [Auto Dispatch](/docs/gameplay/dispatch#set-automatic-priorities).

## Arrange driver meetings

The dispatcher screen includes **Driver Meetings → Schedule Meeting**. Review the meeting's effect, cost, and duration in game before scheduling it. The recorded interface describes meetings taking effect after current deliveries finish.

Use the current meeting offer for the price and effect available to your company.
