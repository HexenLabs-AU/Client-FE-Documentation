## Check your company progress

**Company Progress** shows company level, EXP, and reputation. Use it to see how far you are from your next level; unlocked options depend on your progression.

## Refill the fuel reserve

The **Fuel Reserve** is shared by your trucks. It shows current fuel, capacity, the displayed fuel price, and an estimated range.

1. Open **Headquarters → Overview**.
2. Choose an amount with the slider or the **50%**, **75%**, or **Full** preset.
3. Review the cost shown by **Refuel** before purchasing fuel.

You can choose a fuel payment source under [Financial Management](/docs/gameplay/bank#choose-payment-sources). A [fleet manager](/docs/gameplay/fleet-manager) also offers automatic refueling controls.

## Manage your personal truck

**Assign your vehicle to yourself under Headquarters.** For your first deliveries, assign your Cargo Van here so it becomes **Your Truck** in Dispatch.

The **Your Truck** panel shows the vehicle assigned to you, its location, mileage, overall condition, and the condition of its engine, tires, brakes, and transmission. It also provides **Change Truck** and **Clear** controls.

Choose a **Driving Style**: **Cautious**, **Normal**, or **Aggressive**. Read the comparison for trip time, fuel use, wear, accident risk, police stops, and breakdowns before choosing. This setting is locked while you are on a delivery.

## Relocate your USA headquarters

You can relocate your headquarters **within USA once every seven days**. The relocation price scales with your **company level**. Review the current price before moving.

Relocating within USA is separate from opening or switching to the Mexico branch.

## View branches

The **Branches** tab lets you view your company's **USA and Mexico operations**. **Mexico unlocks at company level 15**, and branch access costs **\$20,000,000**.

**USA and Mexico operations are separate:** each has its own balances, vehicles, staff, company upgrades, and licenses.

Each branch card shows balances, fleet and driver counts, reputation, deliveries, mileage, revenue, and company value. Use **Switch to Branch** on an owned branch to switch between your USA and Mexico operations. The main headquarters is identified separately.

## Find stored items

**Inventory is shared between USA and Mexico, but items cannot be redeemed in Mexico. Switch to USA to redeem them.**

**Storage** has **All**, **Crates**, and **Cosmetics** filters. Use the filters to locate items already in your inventory.

For Halloween skins, select the owned skin in Storage, then choose a truck from its selection panel. Each skin paints one truck and is consumed when applied. See [Halloween truck skins](/docs/events/halloween#apply-a-halloween-truck-skin) for the illustrated guide.

Purchased Halloween drivers are redeemed from inventory. **Keep an empty driver slot before redeeming one.** See [Halloween driver redemption](/docs/events/halloween#redeem-your-halloween-driver).
