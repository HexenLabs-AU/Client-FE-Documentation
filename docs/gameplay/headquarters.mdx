Your headquarters is the place to check your company's progress and shared resources. Open **Headquarters** to switch between **Overview**, **Branches**, and **Storage**.

## Check your company progress

**Company Progress** shows company level, EXP, and reputation. Use it to see how far you are from your next level; unlocked options depend on your progression.

## Refill the fuel reserve

The **Fuel Reserve** is shared by your trucks. It shows current fuel, capacity, the displayed fuel price, and an estimated range.

1. Open **Headquarters → Overview**.
2. Choose an amount with the slider or the **50%**, **75%**, or **Full** preset.
3. Review the cost shown by **Refuel** before purchasing fuel.

You can choose a fuel payment source under [Financial Management](/docs/gameplay/bank#choose-payment-sources). A [fleet manager](/docs/gameplay/fleet-manager) also offers automatic refueling controls.

## Manage your personal truck

The **Your Truck** panel shows the vehicle assigned to you, its location, mileage, overall condition, and the condition of its engine, tires, brakes, and transmission. It also provides **Change Truck** and **Clear** controls.

Choose a **Driving Style**: **Cautious**, **Normal**, or **Aggressive**. Read the comparison for trip time, fuel use, wear, accident risk, police stops, and breakdowns before choosing. This setting is locked while you are on a delivery.

## View branches

The **Branches** tab separates company locations and their progress. Branch cards show balances, fleet and driver counts, reputation, deliveries, mileage, revenue, and company value. An owned branch has a **Switch to Branch** control; the main headquarters is identified separately.

## Find stored items

**Storage** has **All**, **Crates**, and **Cosmetics** filters. Use the filters to locate items already in your inventory.
