## Set corporate laws

The screen includes a freight specialty, **Delivery Tax**, **Company Fuel Card**, and **Company Toll Card**.

Delivery tax takes a chosen share of member earnings, with Federal Contract earnings excluded under the developer's supplied rules.

When enabled, **Company Fuel Card covers all member fuel costs** and **Company Toll Card covers all member toll costs**, using corporate funds. Coverage is not limited to corporation-contract deliveries.

## Manage membership

Use the invite control to invite a company by name. **Join Rules** offers **Open** or **Invite Only**, and **Level Requirement** sets an entry requirement.

The member list offers role controls, **Kick**, and **Make CEO** where your permissions allow them. Review the recipient and action carefully before changing leadership or removing a member.

**Leave Corporation** and **Disband** are separate actions. Outstanding corporation loans restrict leaving or disbanding under the developer's supplied rules.

## Configure role permissions

The role tabs are **Executive**, **Manager**, **Member**, and **Recruit**. The interface states that the CEO can do everything.

Permissions cover inviting and removing members, changing ranks and settings, approving loans, managing businesses, buying upgrades, hauling corporation work, listing businesses, and buying businesses or making offers.

Assign only the permissions you want each role to have; do not assume another corporation uses the same defaults.

## Communicate and contribute

**Board** offers preset quick-chat messages for coordination across servers. Select a message to send it to the corporation. The recorded screen says board messages last 24 hours.

**Main Page → Donate to Corp** contributes company cash to the shared vault. The main page also shows corporation level, reputation, member contributions, loyalty, and member status. Your personal company cash and trucks remain separate from corporate resources.

## Pay member cash bonuses

Corporation CEOs can pay cash bonuses to members **once every 12 hours**.

| Rule | Requirement or limit |
| --- | --- |
| Who can offer bonuses | Corporation CEO. |
| Member eligibility | At least 120 loyalty. |
| Bonus cap | \$5,000,000 per member every 12 hours. |

Meeting the loyalty requirement makes a member eligible for an offered bonus; bonuses are not automatic.

## Review member loans

**Corporation → Loans** shows the vault share available to you, request terms, your loans, pending applications, and activity. Read the displayed eligibility and repayment summary before requesting a loan. Approval requires a permitted manager or the CEO.

**Your borrowing limit is based on tax paid into the corporation and money you donate, capped by the corporation's current bank balance.** Check the available amount in the Loans screen.

**Repayments come from your player's company funds.**

**You request the loan amount and terms, including interest and the repayment period. The CEO or a permitted manager accepts or denies your request.** Review the repayment summary before submitting it.

<Warning>
  **Two failed corporation-loan payments trigger repossession of trucks.** Keep funds available for repayment.
</Warning>

## Review corporation activity

**Corporation → Logs** filters activity by **All**, **Money In**, **Money Out**, **Members**, **Business**, **Contracts**, **Loans**, and **Laws**. It also shows incoming funds, spending, and net movement.

The corporation leaderboard offers experience, reputation, and value comparisons.
