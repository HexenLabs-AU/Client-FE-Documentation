Drivers operate your assigned vehicles and receive a cut of delivery earnings. New players should first assign their Cargo Van to themselves and learn the delivery flow; hire staff when ready to expand. Open **Staff → Drivers** to hire and manage them.

## Hire your first driver

Open **Staff**, roll a **Day Labor** driver, then use **Assign vehicle** underneath to assign your vehicle.

## Hiring differs by region

**Australia and UK players** have a different reroll page. It shows the roll odds, the next **five purchases**, and which driver each purchase will give you.

**Players in other regions receive random drivers when they roll.** The Australia/UK preview screen does not describe the hiring flow for everyone.

Check the hiring price and available roster space before purchasing in either version.

## Compare your drivers

| Detail | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Rarity and stars | Help identify the candidate's tier and progression. |
| Driver Cut | The driver's share of delivery earnings. |
| Speed bonus | Affects delivery speed. |
| Safety bonus | Helps compare driver safety. |
| Fuel bonus | Helps compare fuel performance. |
| Specialty | An additional driver characteristic to review. |

**Day Labor**, **Union Hall**, and **Owner Operator** are separate recruiting lanes. Use the unlock requirements shown in your game. The Australia/UK version provides the odds and purchase previews described above.

The shown Australia/UK interface includes **Hire ×5** alongside individual hiring. Check your available roster space and displayed cost first.

## Read the roster

The roster separates hired, on-duty, and off-duty staff. Driver rows show their assigned truck, status, current job, and completed runs. Use **Dispatch** to reach work controls.

**Let Go** removes a staff member through the dismissal flow. Review the confirmation carefully before dismissing someone.

Company upgrades can increase driver space. The optional **Extra Drivers** pass also adds slots; see [Game passes](/docs/gettingstarted/game-passes).
