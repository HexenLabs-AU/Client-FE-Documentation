Drivers operate your assigned vehicles and receive a cut of delivery earnings. New players should first assign their Cargo Van to themselves and learn the delivery flow; hire staff when ready to expand. Open **Staff → Drivers** to hire and manage them.

## Hire your first driver

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open driver hiring">
    Open **Staff → Drivers** and review the **Day Labor** recruiting lane.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the cost and hire">
    Check the hiring price and available roster space, then roll a driver. Hiring previews depend on your region.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign a vehicle">
    Use **Assign vehicle** underneath to give the driver a truck.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Hiring differs by region

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Australia and UK">
    The reroll page shows the roll odds, the next **five purchases**, and which driver each purchase will give you.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Other regions">
    Drivers are random when you roll. The Australia/UK preview does not describe the hiring flow for your region.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

**Hiring is available from the start. Drivers are paid at each pickup.** Check the hiring price and available roster space before purchasing in either version.

## Compare your drivers

| Detail | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Rarity and stars | Help identify the candidate's tier and progression. |
| Driver Cut | The driver's share of delivery earnings. |
| Speed bonus | Affects delivery speed. |
| Safety bonus | Helps compare driver safety. |
| Fuel bonus | Helps compare fuel performance. |
| Specialty | An additional driver characteristic to review. |

**Day Labor**, **Union Hall**, and **Owner Operator** are separate recruiting lanes. Hiring is available immediately; compare the price and candidates in each lane. The Australia/UK version provides the odds and purchase previews described above.

The shown Australia/UK interface includes **Hire ×5** alongside individual hiring. Check your available roster space and displayed cost first.

## Reassign a truck or replace a driver

Wait for the driver to finish their current load, then **Unassign** the vehicle. You can then assign that truck to the driver you want to use.

## Reroll specialties

Select a driver in **Staff → Drivers** to open their details. Review the **Specialties** section and use **Lock** on specialties you want to keep before rerolling.

**Locking specialties makes rerolls more expensive.** Check the displayed cost before rolling. The screenshot shows the driver-details panel and Lock control; its driver stats are examples.

## Read the roster

The roster separates hired, on-duty, and off-duty staff. Driver rows show their assigned truck, status, current job, and completed runs. Use **Dispatch** to reach work controls.

**Let Go** removes a staff member through the dismissal flow. Review the confirmation carefully before dismissing someone.

Company upgrades can increase driver space. The optional **Extra Drivers** pass also adds slots; see [Game passes](/docs/gettingstarted/game-passes).
