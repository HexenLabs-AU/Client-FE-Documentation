The **Marketplace** contains **Rent Trucks** and **Player Auction**. These are player listings, so compare the individual truck and terms carefully.

**Any vehicle type can be rented out or auctioned, including Cargo Vans.**

## Compare rental listings

Open **Marketplace → Rent Trucks → Available Rentals**. Use size and cargo filters to narrow the list.

| Listing detail | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| Equipment and capacity | Whether the truck can carry the freight you want. |
| Speed and MPG | Travel and fuel performance. |
| Mileage and condition | Wear and potential maintenance costs. |
| Accident and delivery history | The truck's recorded history. |
| You Keep / Owner Takes | How earnings are divided. |
| Term and upfront fee | The displayed duration and cost before renting. |

High-mileage warnings can identify a repair-cost multiplier. Read these before selecting **Rent**.

**My Rentals** and **Rental History** help track rented vehicles. **Post a Truck** and **My Listings** are the controls for managing your own listings.

**Unaccepted rental listings expire after six hours.** This is the listing duration, separate from the rental term after a player rents the vehicle.

## Understand rental terms and responsibilities

**The owner chooses the rental duration.** Check the individual listing rather than assuming every rental lasts the same time.

The supplied **Post a Truck for Rent** form offers:

| Rental term | Real duration |
| --- | --- |
| 2 game years | 24 hours. |
| 3 game years | 36 hours. |
| 4 game years | 48 hours. |
| 5 game years | 60 hours. |
| 6 game years | 72 hours. |

One game year equals 12 real hours. The renter pays the owner **5% of the dealership price upfront**, then the owner's selected share from deliveries. The shown share choices are **5%, 7.5%, 10%, 12.5%, or 15%**.

| Renter pays | Owner accepts |
| --- | --- |
| Upfront contract fee, fuel, maintenance, repairs, and accident costs. | Engine rebuilding, mileage, wear, and depreciation. The truck returns as it is. |

**The lister pays for engine rebuilding; the renter pays accident costs.** Rental duration, listing expiry, and idle-return timing are separate concepts.

## Track returns and return early

Rental cards show **Returns to Owner In** for the rental term and a separate **Idle Return In** countdown. **Assigning the rental resets its idle timer.** If the rental expires during a delivery, **the truck finishes that delivery before returning to its owner**. Check both timers when managing a rental.

Returning a rental early has a fee paid to the owner. Review the displayed fee before confirming; the screenshot's \$1,040 fee is an example for that truck.

## Compare auction listings

Open **Marketplace → Player Auction**. Listings show the auction's remaining time, seller, vehicle details, mileage, condition, delivery and accident history, starting price, fair value, and minimum bid.

Use **Details** to inspect a truck and **Bid** to begin the bidding flow. Review the bid confirmation before committing funds.

**Bid funds are deducted immediately when you bid. If another player outbids you, your bid is refunded.** The seller pays the listing fees; check the displayed fee before listing a truck.
