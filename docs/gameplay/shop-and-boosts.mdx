title: "Shop and boosts"

---

The **Shop** separates **Passes**, **Cash**, and **Boosts**. These are optional purchases; check the current Roblox purchase prompt for the price before confirming.

## Passes

Passes add ongoing features. See [Game passes](/docs/gettingstarted/game-passes) for the benefits shown in the recording.

## Cash packs

The recorded cash-pack labels are **Fuel Money**, **Payroll Cover**, **Fleet Fund**, and **Empire Chest**. Review the amount received and the current Robux price on the pack you choose.

## Temporary boosts

| Boost | Effect | Time added per regular shop purchase |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2× Delivery Speed | Faster deliveries. | 30 minutes. |
| 2× Payout | Double delivery pay. | 30 minutes. |
| 2× EXP | Double EXP. | 30 minutes. |

Check the current timer before buying or claiming a boost.

<Warning>
  Boost timers keep running while you are offline. Repeated boosts extend the timer, including purchased boosts and reward boosts.
</Warning>

Reward codes can also grant temporary boosts. See [Codes](/docs/gettingstarted/codes).
