title: "Shop and boosts"
description: "Find optional passes, cash packs, and temporary boosts."
---

The **Shop** separates **Passes**, **Cash**, and **Boosts**. These are optional purchases; check the current Roblox purchase prompt for the price before confirming.

## Passes

Passes add ongoing features. See [Game passes](/docs/gettingstarted/game-passes) for the available benefits.

## Cash packs

Cash packs include **Fuel Money**, **Payroll Cover**, **Fleet Fund**, and **Empire Chest**. Review the amount received and the current Robux price on the pack you choose.

## Temporary boosts

The **Boosts** tab contains **2× Delivery Speed**, **2× Payout**, and **2× EXP**. **Each regular shop-boost purchase adds 30 minutes.** Check the current timer before buying or claiming a boost.

**Repeated boosts extend the timer, regardless of how you purchase or obtain them.** This includes purchased boosts and boosts received as rewards. **Boost timers keep running while you are offline**, so logging out does not pause them.

Reward codes can also grant temporary boosts. See [Codes](/docs/gettingstarted/codes).
