Cash and Bank are separate balances. Open **Bank** to move money, choose payment sources, or borrow for your company.

## Deposit or withdraw funds

1. Choose **Deposit Cash into Bank** or **Withdraw to Cash**.
2. Enter an amount, or use **MAX**.
3. Check the fee and resulting balance, then confirm.

Deposits have a **3% fee**. Withdrawals are free.

### Earn savings interest

Bank funds earn **1% every three real hours** on the first **\$50,000,000**, including while you are offline. Money above that cap earns no additional interest. The panel shows the countdown to the next payment.

## Choose payment sources

Open **Bank → Financial Management** and choose **Cash** or **Bank** for each expense.

| Payment type | How funds are taken |
| --- | --- |
| BUY | Uses only the selected source. |
| AUTO | Uses the selected source first, then the other source. |

The rows cover purchases, upgrades, headquarters, repairs, rebuilds, fuel, tolls, staff costs, and debt payments. Check **Loan & Financing Payments** before leaving the game.

<Frame caption="Choose which balance pays each expense.">

<Warning>
  Loan and financing payments continue for your entire time offline, with no offline time cap. They follow your payment configuration. Offline delivery earnings stay in escrow until you return and claim them, so they cannot cover these payments.
</Warning>

## Borrowing and truck financing

### Business loan offers

These three offers are fixed. Company upgrades do not change their amounts or rates.

| Loan | Company level | Amount | Interest | Term | Monthly payment |
| --- | --: | --: | --: | --- | --: |
| Small Business Loan | 3 | \$100,000 | 6% | 12 months | \$8,834 |
| Fleet Expansion Loan | 6 | \$600,000 | 8% | 24 months | \$27,000 |
| Commercial Loan | 8 | \$2,500,000 | 10% | 36 months | \$76,389 |

**One game month equals one real hour.** Review **Monthly Payment** and **Total Repaid** before selecting **Apply**.

Interest is calculated upfront and added to the total. For example, borrowing \$100,000 at 6% means repaying \$106,000 in total.

### Truck financing

Financing is available for **new dealership vehicles** and requires a **minimum credit score of 500**. Used vehicles and auction purchases cannot be financed.

New companies start with **three financing slots**, increasing to a maximum of **eight** through company levels and Expanded Credit upgrades. Review the vehicle offer's down payment, installments, interest, term, and total repayment before purchasing.

### Repay early

You can repay loans and financing early, but **the full precalculated interest remains payable**.

### Missed payments and asset seizure

<Warning>
  Missing **two consecutive loan or financing payments** causes trucks to be repossessed to cover the loan. There is no set order for which trucks are taken. This can happen offline.
</Warning>

## Branch transfers

USA and Mexico have separate balances. Open **Send Money to Mexico** in the USA Bank.

| Method | Fee | Arrival |
| --- | --: | --- |
| Armored Truck | 15% | After the displayed travel time. |
| Instant | 35% | Immediately. |

You can send **up to US\$100,000,000 per transfer**, with **one transfer every 35 minutes**.

1. Select a method.
2. Enter the amount, or use **MAX**.
3. Review the fee and currency-conversion preview before sending.

**Transfers only go from USA to Mexico. You cannot send money back to USA.**

The armored truck leaves **New York** and can be followed on the Map. **Recent Transfers** shows the last seven days.
