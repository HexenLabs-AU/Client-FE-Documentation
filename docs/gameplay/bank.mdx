## Deposit or withdraw funds

The main Bank screen shows **Available Cash** and **Banked**.

1. Choose **Deposit Cash into Bank** or **Withdraw to Cash**.
2. Enter an amount, or use **MAX**.
3. Review the displayed fee and resulting balance before confirming.

**Deposits have a 3% fee. Withdrawals are free.** Review the amount credited after the deposit fee before confirming.

### Earn savings interest

Bank funds earn **1% every three real hours**, on the **first \$50,000,000** of your bank balance. Money above that cap does not earn additional interest.

**Savings interest continues while you are offline.** The Bank panel shows a countdown to the next interest payment.

## Choose payment sources

Open **Bank → Financial Management**. Each expense category has **Cash** and **Bank** controls.

| Payment type | How funds are taken |
| --- | --- |
| BUY | Uses only the selected source. |
| AUTO | Uses the selected source first, then the other source. |

Available categories include truck purchases, truck upgrades, company upgrades and headquarters, repairs and rebuilds, fuel, tolls, staff salaries, hiring, and loan or finance payments. Review each row so a purchase or recurring bill uses the funds you intend.

  <img

<Warning>
  Loan and financing payments continue for your entire time offline, with no time cap. They follow your Financial Management selection, including fallback for automatic payments. Offline delivery earnings stay in escrow until you return and claim them, so they cannot cover these payments.
</Warning>

Before logging out, review **Loan & Financing Payments** and keep enough accessible funds for the time you expect to be away.

## Read financial performance

**Financial Overview** shows cash balance, revenue, expenses, net profit, outstanding debt, and company value. Use **View Breakdown** to inspect a total or **Refresh** to update the view.

Choose **1 Day**, **7 Days**, or **30 Days** to compare performance. Graphs show revenue, expenses, profit, cash, and value over the selected period.

**Recent Transactions** and **View All** help explain changes in your balances.

## Borrowing and truck financing

The Bank screen includes **Business Loans** and **Truck Financing**.

### Business loan offers

**The three business-loan offers are fixed.** Company upgrades do not change their amounts or rates; each offer still has its company-level requirement.

The Bank screen offers:

| Loan | Minimum company level | Amount | Interest |
| --- | --: | --: | --: |
| Small Business Loan | 3 | \$100,000 | 6% |
| Fleet Expansion Loan | 6 | \$600,000 | 8% |
| Commercial Loan | 8 | \$2,500,000 | 10% |

| Loan | Term in game months | Term in real hours | Payment each real hour |
| --- | --: | --: | --: |
| Small Business Loan | 12 | 12 | \$8,834 |
| Fleet Expansion Loan | 24 | 24 | \$27,000 |
| Commercial Loan | 36 | 36 | \$76,389 |

**One game month equals one real hour.** The terms above correspond to 12, 24, and 36 real hours. Review the live offer's **Monthly Payment** and **Total Repaid** before selecting **Apply**.

**Loan interest is calculated upfront and added to the total repayment amount.** It is not an annual rate that keeps adding interest throughout the term.

For example, a \$100,000 Small Business Loan at 6% has \$6,000 of interest, for **\$106,000 total repayment**. Check the offer's displayed monthly payment for the installment amount.

Loan payments follow your Financial Management selection and continue while you are offline.

### Truck financing

**Financing is available only for new vehicles purchased from the dealership. Used vehicles and auction purchases cannot be financed.**

**Truck financing requires a credit score of at least 500.** Check the current offer for the down payment, interest, and repayment term.

**New companies start with three financing slots. The maximum is eight.** **Both company levels and Expanded Credit upgrades add financing slots.** Check your current slot count in the Bank.

Check your available slots, monthly installments, and the actual vehicle offer before committing.

Use your live offer for financing rates, terms, and balances.

### Repay early

**You can repay loans and truck financing early. The full precalculated interest remains payable**, so paying early does not reduce that interest.

### Missed payments and asset seizure

<Warning>
  **Miss two consecutive loan or financing payments and your trucks are taken to cover the loan. There is no set seizure order.** This can happen while you are offline because scheduled payments keep running.
</Warning>

Review your Financial Management payment sources and keep enough accessible funds available before leaving the game.

## Branch transfers

USA and Mexico have separate balances. In the USA Bank, use **Send Money to Mexico** to fund the Mexico operation.

| Method | Fee | Arrival |
| --- | --: | --- |
| Armored Truck | 15% | After the displayed travel time. |
| Instant | 35% | Instant transfer. |

You can send **up to US\$100,000,000 per transfer**, with **one transfer every 35 minutes**.

1. Select the transfer method.
2. Enter the amount, or use **MAX**.
3. Review the fee and currency-conversion preview before sending.

**Transfers are one-way: USA to Mexico. You cannot transfer money from Mexico to USA.**

The armored truck leaves the bank in **New York** and can be followed on the Map. **Recent Transfers** shows the last seven days of transfer history.
