Cash and Bank are separate balances. Open **Bank** to move money, review borrowing and truck financing, or check company performance.

## Deposit or withdraw funds

The main Bank screen shows **Available Cash** and **Banked**.

1. Choose **Deposit Cash into Bank** or **Withdraw to Cash**.
2. Enter an amount, or use **MAX**.
3. Review the displayed fee and resulting balance before confirming.

The interface identifies withdrawals as having no fee and describes interest on deposited funds. Check the terms shown in your game before moving money.

## Choose payment sources

Open **Bank → Financial Management**. Each expense category has **Cash** and **Bank** controls.

The screen distinguishes:

- **BUY**: uses only the selected source.
- **AUTO**: uses the selected source first, then the other source.

Available categories include truck purchases, truck upgrades, company upgrades and headquarters, repairs and rebuilds, fuel, tolls, staff salaries, hiring, and loan or finance payments. Review each row so a purchase or recurring bill uses the funds you intend.

<Warning>
  Offline earnings go into escrow until you return and claim them. Do not rely on unclaimed earnings to pay debt. Keep a reserve for payments while you are away; offline loan and finance charges continue throughout your absence.
</Warning>

The exact interaction between offline debt payments and the new source controls is awaiting confirmation. The [FAQ](/docs/gettingstarted/faq) includes the developer's existing Bank-reserve guidance.

## Read financial performance

**Financial Overview** shows cash balance, revenue, expenses, net profit, outstanding debt, and company value. Use **View Breakdown** to inspect a total or **Refresh** to update the view.

Choose **1 Day**, **7 Days**, or **30 Days** to compare performance. Graphs show revenue, expenses, profit, cash, and value over the selected period.

**Recent Transactions** and **View All** help explain changes in your balances.

## Borrowing and truck financing

The Bank screen includes **Business Loans** and **Truck Financing**. Borrowing requirements depend on progression. Check the eligibility and repayment terms on each offer.

**Truck financing requires a credit score of at least 500.** Availability and financing slots also vary with **company level and upgrades**.

Check your financing rating, available slots, monthly installments, and the actual vehicle offer. Paying on time helps your rating; missed payments can reduce it.

Do not use the rates, limits, or balances in the recording as a guarantee for your own company.

## Branch transfers

When a Mexico branch is available, the Bank screen includes **Send Money to Mexico**, transfer choices, a conversion preview, and recent transfer history. Review the fee and timing before selecting a method.
