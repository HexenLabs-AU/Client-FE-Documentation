Some states charge tolls. Your total charge depends on the toll rate and the number of axles on your truck and trailer.

## Toll prices

**Every price below is per axle.**

| Route or state | Price per axle |
| --- | ---: |
| Pennsylvania Turnpike | $60.80 |
| New Jersey Turnpike | $49.40 |
| New York Thruway | $45.60 |
| Illinois, Ohio, Indiana, Florida | $38.00 |
| Massachusetts, Maryland, Kansas, Oklahoma, Texas | $30.40 |
| Maine, West Virginia, Delaware | $22.80 |
| New Hampshire | $19.00 |

## Count your axles

Each row of wheels across your truck counts as one axle. Include the trailer's axles in your total.

**Count axles, rather than individual tires.**

For example, a truck with three axles pulling a trailer with two axles has **five axles total**.

## Calculate a toll

**Total toll = price per axle × total axles**

For a five-axle truck and trailer using the Pennsylvania Turnpike:

**\$60.80 × 5 = \$304.00**

## Avoid tolls

You can choose to avoid tolls, but your trip will take longer.

Corporations can also pay for tolls. See [Corporations](/docs/gameplay/corporations) for an overview of corporation management.
