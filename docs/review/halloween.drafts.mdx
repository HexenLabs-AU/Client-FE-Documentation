## Confirmed from the supplied screenshots

- Pumpkin and Ghost skins cost 400 Candy each and each paints one truck.
- The Legendary Halloween Driver is listed for 1,500 Candy.
- Speed, payout, and EXP boosts cost 150, 200, and 125 Candy respectively and last 30 minutes.
- Up to two of each boost can be purchased per day.
- Owned skins are under Headquarters → Storage; selecting a skin opens a truck picker.
- Candy bundles shown contain 500, 1,200, and 3,000 Candy.
- The screenshot shows a named driver preview. Do not promise the pictured name as every player's reward.

## Confirmed by the developer

- Open the event from the **Map** page.
- The end date is **November 2, 2026, in UTC**. No exact shutdown hour has been supplied; do not infer midnight or add a countdown.
- **Do not disclose Candy earning amounts or rates.** Do not request those numbers.

## Confirmed reward use

- A skin is **consumed when applied** to a truck.
- Players buy a Halloween driver, then **redeem it from inventory**.
- Redeeming a Halloween driver requires an **empty driver slot**.

## Confirmed boost and skin rules

- Repeat boost purchases **extend the timer**.
- The daily boost allowance resets at **00:00 UTC**.
- There is **one skin per vehicle**, and an applied skin **cannot be removed**.

## Confirmed driver specialties

Halloween drivers only roll helpful specialties and cannot receive red drawback specialties. This is confirmed by the developer and the supplied Specialty Odds screenshot.
