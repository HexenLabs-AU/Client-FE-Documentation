## Evidence to review

Source: the supplied UI recording, **Corporation at 00:12–00:47**.

- The original developer notes specify creation cost of \$6,000,000, company level 10; the business purchase requirement is now confirmed below.
- The earlier two-in-house-per-day rule has been superseded by the developer's current confirmation below.
- The Loans UI includes donations + delivery tax, less the member share of corporate spending, in eligible borrowing. This is more specific than the original notes.

## Confirmed timing

**One game month equals one real hour.** Production rates labeled per game month therefore correspond to rates per real hour.

## Confirmed contract posting rules

- In-house posting has **no daily limit**.
- Corporations can have **three active posted contracts in total**, including in-house and open contracts.
- The posting fee is **1% of the contract value**.
- The deadline is **six hours**.
- Contracts sent to the **same other corporation** are limited to **two per 24 hours**.

## Confirmed business upkeep and inactivity

- Upkeep is **2% of business value every 24 hours**.
- A business is **automatically sold after 48 hours without outbound freight**.

## Confirmed business capacity and marketplace

- A corporation must be **level 3** to purchase a business.
- Business capacity starts at **one** and reaches a maximum of **three through upgrades**.
- Scheduled restocks happen **every eight hours**, adding **roughly ten businesses** at a time.
- Other corporations can also list their own businesses for sale.

## New developer confirmations

- Enabled fuel and toll cards cover all member fuel and toll costs.
- Members request corporation loans; the CEO or a permitted manager accepts or denies the request. Loans include interest and repayment terms. Inability to pay leads to repossession. The term-setting party, missed-payment threshold, and exact asset rules remain unspecified; do not import bank-loan default rules.

## Questions for the developer

1. Does production enter storage in one hourly batch or continuously throughout the hour?

3. **Resolved:** enabled corporation fuel and toll cards cover all member fuel and toll costs, rather than only corporation-contract work.
4. Creation cost and minimum company level are confirmed in the original developer notes; corporations set their own entry requirements. Any numeric reputation scoring or cross-branch corporation membership rules remain unspecified.
