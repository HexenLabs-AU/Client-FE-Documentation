**One game month equals one real hour.** Production rates labeled per game month therefore correspond to rates per real hour.

## Confirmed contract posting rules

- In-house posting has **no daily limit**.
- Corporations can have **three active posted contracts in total**, including in-house and open contracts.
- The posting fee is **1% of the contract value**.
- The deadline is **six hours**.
- Contracts sent to the **same other corporation** are limited to **two per 24 hours**.

## Confirmed business upkeep and inactivity

- Upkeep is **2% of business value every 24 hours**.
- A business is **automatically sold after 48 hours without outbound freight**.
