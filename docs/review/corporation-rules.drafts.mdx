## Confirmed rules

- Creation costs \$6,000,000 and requires company level 10. Business purchase requires corporation level 3.
- In-house contracts have no daily posting limit; three posted contracts can be active in total. Posting fee is 1%, deadline six hours, and posting to the same other corporation is limited to two per 24 hours.
- Business capacity starts at one and reaches three through upgrades. Restock is every eight hours with roughly ten businesses, plus player listings.
- Upkeep is 2% every 24 hours. Businesses are automatically sold after 48 hours without outbound freight.
- Production enters the warehouse continuously. One game month is one real hour.
- Enabled fuel and toll cards cover all member fuel and toll costs.
- Borrowing is based on tax paid and donations, capped by the corporation's bank balance. The player requests loan terms; the CEO or permitted manager accepts or denies them.
- Repayments use the player's company funds. Two failed payments trigger truck repossession.
- Mexico does not have corporations yet.
