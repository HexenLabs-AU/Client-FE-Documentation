## Confirmed timing

**One game month equals one real hour.** Production rates labeled per game month therefore correspond to rates per real hour.

## Confirmed contract posting rules

- In-house posting has **no daily limit**.
- Corporations can have **three active posted contracts in total**, including in-house and open contracts.
- The posting fee is **1% of the contract value**.
- The deadline is **six hours**.
- Contracts sent to the **same other corporation** are limited to **two per 24 hours**.

## Confirmed business upkeep and inactivity

- Upkeep is **2% of business value every 24 hours**.
- A business is **automatically sold after 48 hours without outbound freight**.

## Confirmed business capacity and marketplace

- A corporation must be **level 3** to purchase a business.
- Business capacity starts at **one** and reaches a maximum of **three through upgrades**.
- Scheduled restocks happen **every eight hours**, adding **roughly ten businesses** at a time.
- Other corporations can also list their own businesses for sale.

## New developer confirmations

- Enabled fuel and toll cards cover all member fuel and toll costs.
- The developer said the “loaner pays interest and repayment term” and corporation loans also repossess. The term-setting party, missed-payment threshold, and exact asset rules still require clarification. Do not import bank-loan default rules into corporation loans without confirmation.
