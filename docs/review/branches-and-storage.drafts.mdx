## Evidence to review

Source: the supplied UI recording, **Headquarters at 00:06–00:11; Bank at 01:59–02:01**.

- The recording shows an owned US headquarters and Mexico branch, plus branch switching.
- The initial recording had empty Storage. New Halloween screenshots confirm that skins appear in Headquarters → Storage, and selecting an owned skin opens a truck picker.
- Crate opening and other inventory rewards remain unconfirmed.
- Canada is marked Coming Soon in the map.

## Confirmed by the developer

- Branches distinguish USA and Mexico operations.
- Mexico unlocks at company level 15.

1. **Resolved:** Mexico access costs 20,000,000. Balances, vehicles, staff, upgrades, and licenses are separate.
2. The USA-to-Mexico transfer screenshot confirms Armored Truck at 15%, Instant at 35%, up to US\$100,000,000 each, one every 35 minutes, and the live conversion preview. Transfers from Mexico to USA are not available. Armored-truck travel risks remain unconfirmed.
3. Inventory is shared across USA and Mexico, but items cannot be redeemed in Mexico. Confirm which other inventory items are live and how crates are opened.
4. Which regions are currently playable? Keep Canada as a plan until released.
