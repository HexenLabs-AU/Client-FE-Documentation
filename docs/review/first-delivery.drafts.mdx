## Confirmed by the developer

- A new player should buy a Cargo Van and assign the first vehicle to themselves under **Headquarters**.
- In Manual Dispatch, the player's vehicle is represented by Your Truck.
- The final review button is **Dispatch Load**, as shown in the supplied screenshot.
- After dispatch, wait for the load to be delivered. Delivery rewards are credited when it is delivered.
- Loads vary through RNG; a fixed example run or guaranteed freight is not required for the walkthrough.
