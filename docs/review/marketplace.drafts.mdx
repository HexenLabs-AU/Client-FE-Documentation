## Evidence to review

Source: the supplied UI recording, **Marketplace at 01:24–01:30**.

- The rental header and cards show different kinds of terms: upfront fee, rental length, idle return, and owner payout share.
- Historical update-log entries describe listing expiry and early-return fees. These must not be confused with the rental term.

## Confirmed financing restriction

Only new dealership vehicles can be financed. Auction purchases are not eligible for financing.

## Newly confirmed rental and auction rules

- The owner sets the rental duration. The supplied form offers 2–6 game years, or 24–72 real hours.
- The upfront fee is 5% of dealership price, with a selected delivery share.
- The renter pays fuel, maintenance, and repairs. The owner accepts mileage, wear, depreciation, accident risk, and return in the truck's current condition.
- Auction bids deduct money immediately. Outbid bids are refunded.
- The seller pays listing fees.

## Remaining questions

1. Does the owner control only rental duration, or also listing expiry and idle-return timing? Confirm any early-return fee.
2. **Resolved:** any vehicle type can be rented out or auctioned, including Cargo Vans.
3. Who pays rental insurance, rebuilding, and accident liabilities beyond the maintenance and repair responsibilities shown?
4. What happens when the rental ends while its truck is assigned or on a job?
5. Are there auction listing-fee amounts or bid caps that should be documented, or should players use the live listing?
6. A completed rental or bid walkthrough remains optional if a precise confirmation procedure is needed.
