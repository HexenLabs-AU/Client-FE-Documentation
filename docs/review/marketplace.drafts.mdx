## Confirmed rules

- Any vehicle type can be rented out or auctioned. Only new dealership vehicles can be financed.
- Rental listings last six hours. Owner-selected rental terms are separate from listing expiry.
- The renter pays the upfront fee, fuel, maintenance, repairs, and accident costs. The lister pays engine rebuilding.
- The idle timer resets when the rental is assigned. A rental that expires during a delivery returns after that delivery.
- Rental insurance is included. Auction bid funds are deducted immediately and refunded when outbid. Sellers pay listing fees.
- The developer described the early-return fee as half the original price. The original-price basis is not yet explicit, so the public guide directs players to the displayed fee.
