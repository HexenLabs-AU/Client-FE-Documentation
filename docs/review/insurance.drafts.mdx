## Confirmed rules

- Repair and cargo coverage has a one-real-hour waiting period. Switching plans interrupts coverage.
- Claims are automatic; rental vehicles are included.
- Premiums follow Financial Management settings. The insurer drops coverage if the player cannot afford the premium.
- Risk combines fleet risk, driver safety, accident history, and traffic record. At 88 or above, renewal stops.
- Detailed formulas, caps, and plan percentages are outside the beginner-help scope. Use the live plan's terms.
