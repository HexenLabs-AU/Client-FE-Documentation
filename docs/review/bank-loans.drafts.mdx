## Evidence to review

Source: the supplied UI recording, **Bank at 01:55–02:03**.

- Small Business Loan, Fleet Expansion Loan, and Commercial Loan cards are visible.
- The developer's follow-up confirms the deposit fee and upfront loan interest; the supplied Bank balance screenshot confirms savings interest and its cap.

## Confirmed by the developer

- Loan and financing payments use the Financial Management selection, including automatic fallback, while offline.
- They continue throughout offline time without pausing or an offline time cap.
- Offline earnings remain in escrow until claimed and cannot fund payments while unclaimed.

## Confirmed by the supplied loan screenshot

| Loan | Minimum company level | Amount | Interest shown | Term | Monthly payment |
| --- | --: | --: | --: | --- | --: |
| Small Business Loan | 3 | \$100,000 | 6% | 12 months | \$8,834 |
| Fleet Expansion Loan | 6 | \$600,000 | 8% | 24 months | \$27,000 |
| Commercial Loan | 8 | \$2,500,000 | 10% | 36 months | \$76,389 |

The developer confirmed **one game month equals one real hour**. The screenshot labels rates as Interest, not APR.

## Confirmed loan interest and bank savings

- Loan interest is precalculated and added to the total repayment amount.
- Bank deposits have a **3% fee**.
- Bank savings earn **1% every three real hours**, on the **first \$50,000,000** of the bank balance, including while offline.
- Missing **two consecutive loan or financing payments** causes **trucks** to be taken to cover the loan. There is **no set seizure order**.
- Loans and financing can be repaid early, but the **full precalculated interest remains payable**.
- Withdrawals are free, as shown in the Bank interface.

## Review status

The developer confirmed that the three bank-loan offers are **fixed**. Upgrades do not change the offer amounts or rates. No unanswered bank-rule questions remain on this page.
