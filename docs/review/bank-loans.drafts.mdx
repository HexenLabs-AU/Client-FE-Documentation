## Evidence to review

Source: the supplied UI recording, **Bank at 01:55–02:03**.

- Small Business Loan, Fleet Expansion Loan, and Commercial Loan cards are visible.

## Confirmed by the developer

- Loan and financing payments use the Financial Management selection, including automatic fallback, while offline.
- They continue throughout offline time without pausing or an offline time cap.
- Offline earnings remain in escrow until claimed and cannot fund payments while unclaimed.
