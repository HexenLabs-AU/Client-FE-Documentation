## Confirmed by the developer

- Truck financing requires a credit score of at least 500.
- New companies start with **three financing slots**; the maximum is **eight**.
- **Both company levels and Expanded Credit upgrades add slots**.
- Financing is **only for new vehicles purchased from the dealership**. Used and auction vehicles are not eligible.
- Missing **two consecutive loan or financing payments** causes **trucks** to be taken to cover the loan, with **no set seizure order**.
- Loans and financing can be repaid early, but the **full precalculated interest remains payable**.

## Documentation scope

The only credit-score information to publish is the **minimum of 500 required to finance**. Do not request or list score bands, score-based offer explanations, or rating-change rules. Direct players to their current in-game offer.
