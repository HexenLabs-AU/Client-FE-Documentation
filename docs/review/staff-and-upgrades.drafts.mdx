## Confirmed rules

- Hiring is available immediately. Drivers are paid per pickup; other staff are paid every game month (one real hour).
- Wait for a driver to finish a load, then unassign their truck before reassigning it to another driver.
- Driver details contain specialty controls. Locking wanted specialties increases reroll cost.
- Open Fleet → My Vehicles and select a truck to find rebuilding when needed. No rebuild screenshot was supplied because the developer has no truck that needs it.
- One escort is required per Lowboy. Without an available escort, larger loads are unavailable and earning opportunities fall.
- Automated maintenance works offline and covers personal vehicles. Renter maintenance responsibilities are described in the rental guide.
- Advanced Dispatcher controls and their commitment-filter exceptions are documented from supplied screenshots. Australia/UK hiring previews are regional.
- Detailed hiring odds, salary formulas, upgrade tiers, and price scaling are outside the current beginner-help scope.

## Confirmed meeting rules

- Safety Meeting: Safety Performance +25%, with fewer accidents and traffic stops, for two hours.
- Maintenance Meeting: Maintenance Performance +25%, with fewer breakdowns and less wear, for two hours.
- Incentive Meeting: Revenue Performance +5%, with more money per run, for two hours.
- Meeting effects do not stack. Check the live cost; the screenshot's price is an example.
