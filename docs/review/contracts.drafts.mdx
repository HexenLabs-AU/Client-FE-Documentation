## Evidence to review

Source: the supplied UI recording, **Contracts at 01:46–01:54; Dispatch at 01:17–01:23**.

- Business offers show relationship-dependent work; Federal listings show deposit, level, fleet-capacity, and participant requirements.
- Auto Dispatch wording about contract precedence may differ from the historical driver-priority fix.

## Confirmed by the developer

- Federal Contracts pay only at the end of the contract, rather than after each load.
- The final payout is based on how many loads the company completed during the contract.

## New developer confirmation

- No contracts work offline.
- Federal deposits are returned when the contract is completed. This is documented in the public guide; failed contracts pay nothing, there is no partial payout, and the deposit is lost.

## Questions for the developer

1. Confirm relationship thresholds, offer-roll timing, negotiation options, and maximum accepted deals.
2. Confirm Federal Contract roll costs, odds, unlocks, joining requirements, deposit amounts, and deadlines. Failure and deposit-loss rules are resolved above.
3. **Resolved:** no contracts progress offline, including business deals, Federal Contracts, and corporation work.
4. Do contracts always override driver priorities, or only with particular rules enabled?
5. Provide one roll/join/accept/complete flow and a relationship expansion screen.
