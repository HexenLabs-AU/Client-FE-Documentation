## Confirmed rules

- Business offers and negotiation work through posting freight. Exact business-posting button sequences were not supplied; the public guide uses the observed Offers controls.
- Automatic contract work requires Dispatch Priority set to Contracts.
- No contract work progresses offline.
- Federal screenshots confirm Roll Contracts, Reroll, offer review, joining settings, Post to the Board, and Start Contract.
- Posting recruits partners without taking the deposit or starting the delivery clock. Start Contract takes the deposit and starts the offer's time limit.
- The recruiting screen shows cancellation without loss to joined participants and a listing-expiry countdown separate from the delivery timer.
- Successful contracts pay at the end, split by hauled freight, and return the deposit. Failed Federal Contracts have no partial payout and lose the deposit.
- Offer amounts, level requirements, odds, and workload are live offer values, not universal numbers.
