Spend Candy on Halloween truck skins, a seasonal Legendary driver, and delivery boosts.

## Join the event

Open the **Map** page to access the **Halloween Event** and its shop.

**The event ends on November 2, 2026 (UTC).**

## Pick your Halloween reward

| Reward | Cost | What you get |
| --- | --: | --- |
| Pumpkin skin | 400 Candy | Orange-and-black pumpkin design for one truck. |
| Ghost skin | 400 Candy | Purple ghost design for one truck. |
| Halloween driver | 1,500 Candy | A seasonal Legendary driver. |

Candy is the event currency. The Halloween Shop shows each reward's cost, your owned quantity, and whether you have enough Candy to purchase it. Quantity controls let you select how many skins or drivers to buy.

The shop also notes that company drivers earn Candy while you are away.

Driver previews vary. See [Drivers](/docs/gameplay/drivers) for regional hiring differences.

## Halloween driver specialties

**Halloween drivers only receive helpful specialties. They cannot receive red drawback specialties.** You can review the current specialties in the driver's details.

## Redeem your Halloween driver

Buying a Halloween driver adds it to your **shared inventory**. **Switch to USA before redeeming it**; inventory items cannot be redeemed in Mexico. Open **Headquarters → Storage** when you are ready to add it to your driver roster.

<Warning>
  **You must have an empty driver slot to redeem the Halloween driver.** Check your roster space before redeeming the inventory item.
</Warning>

## Boost your deliveries

| Boost | Cost | Effect | Duration |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| 2× Speed | 150 Candy | Faster deliveries. | 30 minutes. |
| 2× Payout | 200 Candy | Double delivery pay. | 30 minutes. |
| 2× EXP | 125 Candy | Double EXP. | 30 minutes. |

You can buy **up to two of each boost per day**. The daily allowance resets at **00:00 UTC**.

**Repeat purchases extend the boost timer. Boost timers keep running while you are offline.** Check your remaining time and daily allowance before buying again.

## Apply a Halloween truck skin

Each vehicle has **one skin**. A skin is **consumed when applied** and **cannot be removed** from the vehicle. Owned skin items appear in **Headquarters → Storage**. **Switch to USA to redeem inventory items; they cannot be redeemed in Mexico.**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get a skin from the Halloween Shop">
    Choose **Pumpkin skin** or **Ghost skin** and the quantity you want. Each costs **400 Candy**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find your skin">
    Open **Headquarters → Storage**. Use **All** or **Cosmetics** to find the owned skin.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the item">
    Select the skin. For example, selecting **Pumpkin** opens **Use Pumpkin Skin**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a truck">
    Choose a truck from the selection panel to apply the skin to that vehicle. Applying it consumes the skin item.
  </Step>
</Steps>
