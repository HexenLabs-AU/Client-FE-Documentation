## Build your company

- **Manage your fleet.** Buy trucks and build a fleet for your transportation company.
- **Move freight.** Find loads, complete deliveries, and grow your business.
- **Hire drivers.** Recruit and manage the team that keeps your company moving.
- **Build relationships.** Work with other businesses and unlock contracts.
- **Join a corporation.** Team up with other companies and work toward shared goals.
- **Rent trucks.** Expand your fleet through vehicle rentals.
- **Compete.** Climb the leaderboards as your company grows.
